## Phase 21 — v1.0 migration
v1.0 is stable upstream since 2026-11-06 (upstream #3999). Waiting on the npm release of `@a2ui/react` that supports v1.0 stably: the fix that moves the renderer's own source off `@a2ui/web_core/v0_9` and makes it type-check on v1.0 surfaces is on upstream `main` (upstream #3423, closed 2026-11-12 by #4181) and unreleased. There will be no `@a2ui/react/v1_0`; the package root is the version-agnostic entry point, and the v1.0 basic catalog ships in `@a2ui/web_core/catalogs/basic/v1`. The v1.0 component envelope is mid-cutover upstream: `@catalogId`, `@metadata`, `@accessibility` and `@checks` replace the bare keys (#4044, merged 2026-11-11, unreleased); `@child`, `@action` and `@functionCall` replace bare child ids and action objects (#4171, open, stacked on the dual-read #4170); `updateDataModel.value` becomes required (#3930, open). The wait ends with #4171 landed and the TypeScript and Python releases that carry it. The upstream upgrade as Phase 16 did it, on the releases of `@a2ui/web_core`, `@a2ui/react`, `a2ui-core`, `a2ui-agent-sdk` and `a2ui-a2a` that carry the React fix, the envelope cutover and v1.0's agent and renderer function messages (`callRendererFunction`, `callAgentFunction` and their responses); the function messages shipped for Python on 2026-11-10 (`a2ui-core` 0.6.0, `a2ui-agent-sdk` 0.11.0, `a2ui-a2a` 0.3.0) and are unreleased for TypeScript. Checked 2026-11-12: `@a2ui/web_core` and `@a2ui/react` still 0.15.0 of 2026-11-02, no release PR open; the unreleased `@a2ui/web_core` changelog holds 15 breaking entries, 11 of them on every protocol version, and the skipped 0.15.0 five more. The migration checklist for agents, renderers and catalog authors: `specification/v1_0/docs/evolution_guide.md` §3. The version boundary in the codebase: what belongs to v1.0, what to v0.9 and v0.9.1, and the parts the two share. One running A2UIVerse speaks one side of the boundary: v1.0 by default, v0.9 and v0.9.1 by a switch at launch. The delta register written in v1.0's vocabulary. Until then A2UIVerse works on v0.9 and v0.9.1.
