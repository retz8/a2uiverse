# 18.3 — The sample beside the platform

The restaurant finder from `upstream/main`, run unchanged the way its README says, fetched, installed, asked once through the hub and pressed inside, before the dry run. Sub-task 18.3 of Phase 18 (`_dev/docs/spec/phase-18-ecosystem-run.md`, decisions 1, 10 and 12). Parallel with 18.1 and 18.2, before 18.4.

## Scope

- The Python ADK restaurant finder running from a worktree of the fork at `upstream/main`, on its default model.
- Its card fetched and the app installed from its card URL alone, nothing packed.
- The Store's judgement of its first paint, run on the Mac.
- One question routed to it through the hub, and the booking inside its fragment through to the confirmation.
- Whatever is found, recorded in its register; the fallback to the Node restaurant finder, judged here.
- Out: the trusted-page publish and the marketplace's state, which the dry run exercises; the composition beside Calendar; the recording; any change to the sample.

## Locked decisions

### 1. The door is the registry's, not the marketplace's

The app is installed through the orchestrator's registry command from its card URL, with no marketplace involved and no publisher claimed. The Store's judgement of its first paint — the card fetched, the skill's example asked, the paint checked against the basic catalog — is run on the Mac with Stellify's preview, which touches no marketplace state.

### 2. Port 11006, unforwarded

The sample runs on port 11006, the next slot in the vendor-agent range. The port is not forwarded through the tunnel: the orchestrator and the preview reach it on `localhost`, and the app has no sign-in page.

### 3. The pictures are an environment fact

The sample serves its restaurant pictures from its own `localhost` address, so they are blank in a browser on the far side of the tunnel whatever is forwarded. That is neither a sample finding nor a platform finding: the tunnel doc says why, one request from the Mac proves they are served, and what the recording does about it is the dry run's call.

### 4. The question is the sample's own, asked of it alone

"Find me an Italian restaurant", typed on the canvas with the seven apps installed beside it, the roster in deterministic mode, the Planner and Synthesizer at the phase's defaults. The Planner routes it from the card alone. No two-part question, no Calendar.

### 5. The press runs through to the confirmation

Book on a restaurant, then the booking form filled with what the sample asks for and submitted, the confirmation painted in place. Two presses, the second carrying the form's bound values.

### 6. The real registry, uninstalled after

The app is installed into the registry the launcher keeps today, beside the seven, and uninstalled from it once the stages are done, so the sitting starts as phase decision 6 wants it.

### 7. The id is `restaurant-finder`

The app's id in the registry, the journal and later the sitting's publish form is `restaurant-finder`, the sample's folder name on `upstream/main`.

### 8. The worktree is `../a2ui-upstream/`

A sibling worktree of the fork, detached at `upstream/main`, the fork's own branch untouched; the workspace's structure note says what the folder is. The sample's environment file holds the project's existing Gemini key under the name the sample reads; the model is left at its default.

### 9. 18.3 makes the fallback call

A sample finding that blocks one of the five stages — the card fetched, the install, the paint, Book, the submit — with no platform fix that is right for the product sends the Node restaurant finder through the same five stages in this sub-task, and the 18.4 line is amended to name the finder that goes to the dry run. A sample finding that blocks nothing is recorded and the Python one stays. A gap on the platform's side is an 18.5 sub-task, never a fallback.

### 10. Findings and the tunnel row are the record

A sample finding goes to `_dev/a2ui-findings.md` from number 23 as an upstream finding; a platform finding to the Backlog, or as an 18.5 sub-task when it must land before the sitting. The tunnel doc gains the port's row. The TODO line, when ticked, names the `upstream/main` commit the sample ran from, and the model knob if the default model had to be moved. The preview's output is not kept. No run note; the sitting log is the sitting's.

### 11. Claude in Chrome drives

The turn and the presses are driven in the controlled browser through the tunnel, the same driver as the dry run and the recording; the journal and the logs on the Mac confirm each stage.

## Invariants

- The sample is never changed.
- The platform runs at the phase's defaults.
- The marketplace's state is untouched.
