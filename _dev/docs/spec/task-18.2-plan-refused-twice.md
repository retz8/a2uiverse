The orchestrator's final for a question whose plan the validator refused twice, "the Planner's answer was refused 2 times: …" with the findings after it, said for customers. Sub-task 18.2 of Phase 18 (`_dev/docs/spec/phase-18-ecosystem-run.md`, decision 11); the Backlog's finding from 17.7. Parallel with 18.1 and 18.3, before the dry run.

## Scope

- The words the orchestrator puts on the final of a question whose plan was refused twice.
- Where the validator's findings go once they leave the screen.
- The orchestrator's unit tests for the broken turn.
- The orchestrator design record's passages that say the final names the findings.
- Out: the client, which shows the final's text unchanged; how a failed final is shown and for how long; the Planner's model call failing, whose provider message reaches the screen today — a Backlog line, found in 18.2.

## Locked decisions

### 1. The orchestrator says the line

The final carries the plain line, and the client relays it unchanged, as it does for the press-busy refusal and "This context is closed."

### 2. The line

"A2UIVerse couldn’t work out how to answer that. Ask again, or try other words." A statement followed by what to do, no opener. The apostrophe follows the orchestrator's existing lines.

### 3. One line for both shapes

The same line whether the plan was refused before its dispatch list was committed or after it, when the half-drawn layout is taken down and the apps cancelled.

### 4. The findings on the plan's log line

The plan's existing log line at the refusal carries the last attempt's findings. The journal keeps both attempts with their findings as it does today. The turn's closing log line prints the plain words, as it does for every other failed turn.

### 5. The refused-twice line alone

The Planner's model call failing is not covered here; it is recorded in the Backlog as found in 18.2.

### 6. Verified by unit tests alone

The two orchestrator tests that assert the final's old text are re-pointed at the plain line and assert the findings are no longer in the final; the first is extended to assert the plan's log line carries them. No live look; the dry run in 18.4 is the live check if a refusal happens on the way.

### 7. The records

The orchestrator design record's two passages that state the final names the findings — the Planner section's retry bullet and the failure table's row — are rewritten in the same session. The glossary's "Broken turn", the store-loop design record and the client design record stay as written. The Backlog's line for this finding is dropped when the sub-task is done; the line for the model-call neighbour is added.
